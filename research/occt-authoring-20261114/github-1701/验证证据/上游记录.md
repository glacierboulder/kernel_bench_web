### Description

`BRepFilletAPI_MakeFillet` fillets one concave edge whose end vertex lies on the tangent line of an existing 1.3 mm fillet. With radius 1.3, equal to the existing fillet, it reports success. `BRepCheck_Analyzer` and the `BOPAlgo_ArgumentAnalyzer` curve-on-surface check both pass, but two vertices get a tolerance of 4.83 mm. A later box cut on the result removes the wrong volume and returns an invalid solid. Radii 1.299 and 1.301 give clean results.

The solid is a 10 x 10 x 2.5 plate with a lip on top for y < 5. The lip's front face leans 1 degree and meets the plate in a 1.3 mm root fillet. The filleted edge is where the lip's end face (y = 5) meets the plate top. With the lip face vertical, the same fillet is clean.

Found on V8.0.1; not tested on master. The form's version list stops at 7.9, so "latest" below means V8.0.1.

Possibly related: #736 (a fillet that goes wrong only at radius 1.0; 0.99 and 1.01 work).

### Expected Behavior

Either a result with vertex tolerances like those of the neighbouring radii, or `IsDone()` false.
