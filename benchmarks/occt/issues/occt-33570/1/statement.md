# Subtract partially overlapping spheres

Subtract the unit sphere centred at (0, 4.5, 14) from the radius-four sphere centred at (0, 5.5, 18). The result should include the spherical cut face and satisfy the analytic difference volume. The attached reproduction checks the topology.
